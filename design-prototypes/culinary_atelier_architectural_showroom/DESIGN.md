## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the stillness, discipline, and uncompromising craft of haute-architecture kitchen ateliers. It balances the dignity of physical marble-and-timber flagships with the precision of contemporary culinary engineering. The target audience comprises discerning private homeowners, interior architects, and luxury developers seeking custom millwork, integrated appliances, and bespoke spatial curation.

The aesthetic fuses **Architectural Minimalism** with **Editorial Fine-Art Publishing**:
- **Gallery Cadence:** Generous, intentional negative space mirrors the pacing of a private exhibition or bespoke atelier walkthrough.
- **Physical Lineage:** Visual weight mimics authentic materiality—brushed bronze joinery, honed limestone, and oiled smoked oak.
- **Dignified Credibility:** Restraint governs every interaction. Elements do not compete; they frame high-resolution architectural photography, elevation diagrams, and material specimens.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in mineral and architectural tones:

- **Primary Background (`#F7F5F0` / Warm Ivory):** The dominant canvas across all viewports. Provides an organic, museum-grade parchment warmth rather than clinical digital white.
- **Primary Text (`#20211F` / Ebony Charcoal):** Deep, warm charcoal delivering high-contrast legibility without the harshness of pure `#000000`.
- **Secondary Background (`#E9E5DE` / Pebble):** Used for structural contrast, inset elevation viewports, technical specification panels, and section dividers.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated card staging, sample mounts, and floating technical dossiers to create crisp layered planes over the ivory base.
- **Accent Bronze (`#A77B4A` / Muted Bronze):** Applied sparingly to bespoke hairline rules, active architectural markers, editorial category indicators, and quiet key accents.
- **Secondary Text (`#73716C` / Stone Grey):** Delivers understated typographic hierarchy for metadata, dimension labels, captions, and technical specifications.
- **Subtle Borders (`#DCD7CE` / Mortar Line):** Used for hairline grid articulation, framing, and architectural boundary markers.
- **Action Emerald (`#25D366` / Direct Concierge):** Strictly insulated for high-conversion concierge outreach and instant WhatsApp atelier consultations. Never used for general branding or decorative UI elements.

## Typography

The typographic balance bridges classical European architectural monographs and modernist technical drafting:

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles (`Playfair Display`):** Carries natural rhythm, high contrast, and sculptural elegance. Used for collection names, exhibition themes, and architectural quotes. Larger sizes should strictly employ standard or slightly negative tracking to retain density.
- **Specifications, Navigation & Reading Body (`Manrope`):** Delivers clean geometry, precise legibility, and architectural neutrality. Pairs cleanly alongside detailed technical dimensions, finish descriptions, and appliance specifications.
- **Labeling & Metadata:** Always rendered in tracked uppercase (`letter-spacing: 0.14em`) via Manrope to function like engraved architectural stamps or technical blueprint callouts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an **Asymmetric Architectural Grid** that prioritizes expansive sightlines over dense data packing.

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 1440px max-width container, 4rem outer canvas margins, and 2rem gutters. Layouts utilize intentional asymmetry (e.g., a 7-column hero elevation offset against a 5-column technical dossier).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Reflows into structured pairings with 2rem margins and 1.5rem gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Converts to a disciplined single-axis flow with 1.25rem outer margins and 1rem gutters, maintaining architectural line breaks.
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Section transitions rely on generous vertical pauses (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`), mimicking the experience of transitioning between physical rooms within a private showroom.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects aggressive drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering** and **Architectural Boundaries**:

- **Planar Staging:** Depth is created through surface contrast: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) modules sit over Warm Ivory (`#F7F5F0`), with recessed panels rendered in Pebble (`#E9E5DE`).
- **Precision Hairlines:** Structural boundaries utilize 1px solid `#DCD7CE`. Panels overlap like precision-cut stone slabs rather than floating software cards.
- **Subtle Atmospheric Ambient:** For floating concierge elements or active overlay drawers, use an imperceptible shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(32, 33, 31, 0.05)`.
- **Frosted Material Insets:** Full-screen inspection overlays apply backdrop-filtered ivory translucency (`rgba(247, 245, 240, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).

## Shapes

The showroom aesthetic mandates absolute architectural discipline:

- **Geometric Purity (Level 0 - Sharp):** UI elements utilize strict 0px border radii. Buttons, card frames, material swatch frames, image viewports, and dialogue panels possess crisp, razor-sharp 90-degree corners.
- **Architectural Reference:** Sharp geometry mirrors precision-cut quartzite countertops, flush cabinet reveals, and architectural millwork lines.
- **Softness via Imagery:** Organic curvature is introduced exclusively through the imagery itself (e.g., fluted glass, sculptural range hoods, and custom culinary fittings).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Atelier CTA:** Sharp-cornered solid `#20211F` container with `#F7F5F0` uppercase text. Hover state shifts subtly to `#A77B4A` via a crisp 200ms ease. Padding: `1rem 2rem`.
- **Secondary Architectural Link:** Hairline 1px border (`#20211F` or `#DCD7CE`), transparent fill, `#20211F` text. Hover state introduces a faint `#E9E5DE` fill.
- **Concierge Direct (WhatsApp):** Solid `#25D366` container with white typography and WhatsApp glyph. Sharp edges preserve architectural alignment while the color commands immediate attention for appointment scheduling.

### Cards & Material Panels
- **Finish & Swatch Showcase:** Flat `#FFFFFF` canvas bordered by 1px `#DCD7CE`. Images bleed to the edge with a 1:1 or 4:5 aspect ratio. Titles are set in Playfair Display with metadata labels set in uppercase Manrope.
- **Hover Micro-interaction:** Image scales subtly (`1.02` transform over 400ms) with no drop shadow shifts.

### Chips & Filtering
- **System Tags:** Minimalist sharp tags with 1px `#DCD7CE` borders, `#F7F5F0` background, and `#73716C` text.
- **Active State:** Solid `#20211F` background with `#F7F5F0` text, or underlined with a 1.5px `#A77B4A` baseline rule.

### Input Fields & Booking Forms
- **Field Styling:** Flat background (`#FFFFFF` or transparent over ivory), featuring a crisp bottom hairline border (`1px solid #DCD7CE`). Focus state turns the baseline border to `#A77B4A` with zero outer glow.
- **Labels:** Persistent uppercase floating labels (`#73716C`) set in `label-uppercase` token styling.

### Lists & Technical Specifications
- **Data Pairs:** Alternating hairline dividers (`#DCD7CE`). Specification parameter left-aligned in `#73716C`, value right-aligned in `#20211F` with `spec-numeral` weight.
- **Editorial Bullet Points:** Replaced by delicate `#A77B4A` square markers (2px by 2px) or custom thin dashes.

### Custom Architectural Components
- **Elevation Dimension Viewer:** Interactive technical schematic module featuring bronze callout pins (`#A77B4A`), toggleable dimension lines, and integrated material swatches.
- **Materiality Drawer:** Lateral slide-out panel set in `#F7F5F0` displaying high-resolution macro photography of stone veins, patinated brass, and timber grains alongside origin documentation.
