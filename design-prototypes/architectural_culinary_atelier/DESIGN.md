## Brand & Style

This design system reflects an architectural luxury standard tailored for high-end kitchen appliances and bespoke culinary interior spaces. The aesthetic merges editorial discipline with tactile warmth, communicating uncompromising precision, culinary passion, and enduring craft.

### Visual Aesthetic & Philosophy
- **Tactile Architectural Minimalism:** Surfaces evoke curated physical showroom stones, unlacquered metals, and matte architectural finishes. Visual weight is derived from deliberate framing, strict alignment, and pristine proportions rather than heavy embellishments.
- **Editorial Poise:** Imagery is showcased with generous, deliberate whitespace. Layouts balance full-bleed exhibition viewports with razor-sharp structural information blocks.
- **Direct Conversion Integrity:** High-intent commercial actions—notably showroom bookings and direct WhatsApp consults—are integrated seamlessly without disrupting the refined spatial elegance.

### Emotional Response
The interface must evoke discernment, calm confidence, effortless competence, and timeless architectural luxury.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in architectural materials: warm limestone, patinated bronze, honed charcoal stone, and pristine gallery white.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Surface (`#F8F6F1` Warm Ivory):** The primary canvas across all viewports. Eliminates harsh digital glare while providing a tactile, organic foundation.
- **Container Surface (`#FFFFFF` Pure White):** Used selectively for active product modules, specification sheets, and elevated exhibition cards.
- **Dominant Base (`#252525` Charcoal):** The primary text color and deep architectural accent. Delivers high-contrast legibility with a softer edge than pure black.
- **Accents & Metallics (`#A77B4A` Muted Bronze):** Used for focal category tags, active states, key specs, and bespoke architectural markers.
- **Structural Lines (`#EAE3D9` Soft Beige):** Hairline borders, dividers, frame bounds, and inactive chip contours.
- **Muted Metadata (`#777777` Secondary Grey):** Secondary descriptive text, technical labels, SKU codes, and footnote specifications.
- **Direct Action (`#25D366` WhatsApp Green):** Reserved exclusively for high-intent communication CTAs and direct concierge triggers.

### Functional States
- **Hover/Interactive Dark:** `#191919` (Charcoal pressed/hover).
- **Bronze Hover:** `#8F683D` (Subtle deepening on interactive state).
- **Subtle Surface Tint:** `#F2EDE4` (Warm Ivory depressed/hover tint).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes an editorial tension between the classical serif authority of Playfair Display and the clean geometric precision of Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Section Headers:** Playfair Display delivers an editorial magazine tone. Headings above 28px utilize tight letter spacing and restrained line heights to feel sculptured and composed.
- **Body & Editorial Narratives:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides frictionless readability at all scales. Generous line heights (`1.55`–`1.6`) maintain visual lightness across technical specification listings.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Displayed exclusively in uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans with expansive letter tracking (`+0.06em` to `+0.1em`), evoking gallery labels and architectural blueprinted metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a strict 12-column architectural grid supported by generous framing boundaries.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container width of 1440px. Outer margins set to `3.5rem` (`56px`), with standard column gutters of `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` (`32px`) margins and `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters. Reflow switches side-by-side appliance specs into progressive stacked panels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` (`20px`) outer margins and `1rem` (`16px`) gutters. Horizontal scrolling rails are used for category exploration and material swatches.

### Spacing Discipline
Spacing follows an intentional vertical rhythm where section dividers are separated by generous breathing room (`space-xl` and higher), allowing premium appliance photography to command focus without competition.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy drop shadows and artificial blurred elevations, embracing flat architectural materiality, razor hairline borders, and subtle tonal step-offs.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Hairline Framing:** Depth is primarily established through `1px solid #EAE3D9` (Soft Beige) borders separating Pure White panels from the Warm Ivory background canvas.
- **Tonal Stepping:** Inactive surfaces reside on `#F8F6F1` (Warm Ivory). Active, interactive, or catalog showcase containers transition to `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
- **Subtle Ambient Grounding:** For floating bottom bars, sticky navigation, and modal inquiry drawers, elevation is achieved via a whisper of diffused shadow:
  - `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(37, 37, 37, 0.05), 0 2px 6px rgba(37, 37, 37, 0.02)`
  - Tinted with primary charcoal to eliminate synthetic grey cast.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is strictly sharp (`roundedness: 0`), referencing contemporary architectural cabinetry, honed monolithic marble islands, and precision-milled steel ranges.

### Structural Discipline
- **Corner Radius:** 0px across all primary containers, buttons, cards, modals, and input fields.
- **Micro-Elements:** Category pills and tags maintain crisp 90-degree corners, reinforced with hairline `1px` borders.
- **Exceptions:** Floating action indicators or standard iconography conform to clean geometric bounds without rounded container bubbles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Brand Charcoal):** Background `#252525`, text `#FFFFFF`, 0px radius, uppercase tracked font (`label-md`). Padding `14px 28px`. Hover: `#191919` with a subtle `1px` inner bronze highlight.
- **Showroom Enquiry / WhatsApp (Direct Conversion):** Background `#25D366`, text `#FFFFFF`, 0px radius, uppercase tracked font (`label-md`). Hover: `#20BA5A`. Includes an integrated direct WhatsApp glyph.
- **Secondary (Editorial Outline):** Background transparent, border `1px solid #252525`, text `#252525`. Hover: background `#252525`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Tertiary Accent (Muted Bronze):** Border `1px solid #A77B4A`, text `#A77B4A`, background transparent. Hover: background `#A77B4A`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Product Cards & Appliance Viewports
- **Structure:** Clean Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) card on Warm Ivory (`#F8F6F1`) backdrop. Framed with a 1px `#EAE3D9` border.
- **Image Staging:** 4:5 or 1:1 aspect ratio with neutral showroom styling. Images feature a subtle inner border and no drop shadow.
- **Metadata Block:** Category in `label-sm` Muted Bronze (`#A77B4A`), appliance title in `headline-sm` Playfair Display (`#252525`), and spec bullets in `body-sm` Secondary Grey (`#777777`).

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default State:** Transparent background, `1px solid #EAE3D9`, text `#777777`, 0px radius, uppercase tracking.
- **Active State:** Pure White background, `1px solid #A77B4A`, text `#A77B4A` with an accompanying subtle left bronze notch.

### Input Fields & Enquiry Forms
- **Field Styling:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #EAE3D9`, text `#252525`, 0px radius. Padding `16px 18px`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts sharply to `1px solid #252525` without fuzzy focus rings.
- **Floating Labels:** Rendered in `label-sm` uppercase, shifting from `#777777` to `#A77B4A` upon focus.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Design:** Geometric squares with sharp 90-degree edges. Unchecked: `1px solid #EAE3D9`, background `#FFFFFF`. Checked: solid `#252525` fill with a crisp white geometric check or center square.

### Specification & Comparison Table
- **Structure:** Minimalist ledger styling. Alternating hairline borders (`1px solid #EAE3D9`), with zero vertical divider lines. Column headers in `label-md` Charcoal, values in `body-md` Secondary Grey.
